Put your product and hero photos here (JPG/PNG/WebP), then reference them from
`js/products.js`:

- Hero slide: `image: "images/hero-shoes.jpg"` on a `HERO_SLIDES` entry
- Product: `image: "images/velvet-stiletto.jpg"` on a product, or per colour on a colorway

Square photos (e.g. 1200×1200) work best for products; wide ones (e.g. 1600×900) for the hero.
If a photo fails to load, the built-in illustration is shown instead.
